**Effective date:** 13 September 2026

Volura ("the app") is developed by Umut Ulaş Demir, an individual developer in
Türkiye ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app uses, where it goes
and the choices you have. Türkçe sürüm: [Gizlilik Politikası](../tr/privacy/).

## Summary

- There is no account to create. We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you
  across other apps and websites.
- Your workouts, programs, profile and measurements are stored on your device and
  in **your own iCloud account**. We cannot see them.
- Data leaves your device for our servers only when you use the **AI Coach**, and
  only after you allow it. Health data is shared with the coach only if you turn
  that on separately.

## Data stored on your device and in your iCloud

The app stores your profile and profile photo, workouts and logged sets, schedule,
custom workouts and programs, saved content, body measurements and preferences.
This data is kept on your device and synchronized through Apple's iCloud (the
private CloudKit database and iCloud key-value storage) so it is available on your
other devices. It is stored under your Apple Account and is not accessible to us.
Coach conversations are stored only on your device.

## Apple Health

With your permission:

- the iPhone app **reads** body measurements, such as weight and body
  composition, to keep your progress up to date;
- the Apple Watch app **reads** heart rate and active energy during a workout and
  **saves** your workouts and active energy to Apple Health.

Health data is used only to provide these features. It is never used for
advertising or sold, and it is not sent to our servers unless you allow the AI
Coach to use health data (see below). You can change these permissions at any
time in the Settings app under Health.

## AI Coach

The AI Coach answers questions and can adjust your plan. Before your first message
leaves the device, the app asks for your permission.

**What is sent, when you use the coach:** your message and the training data the
coach needs to answer, such as workouts, logged sets, your program, goals and
exercise history. **Body measurements, workout heart rate and active calories are
sent only if you separately allow health data**; this is off unless you turn it on.

**Where it goes:** requests pass through our server, which runs on Cloudflare, and
are forwarded to **OpenAI** to generate the reply. OpenAI processes this data under
its API terms and policies; see
[openai.com/policies](https://openai.com/policies).

**What our server keeps:**

- a random install identifier and, on supported devices, an Apple App Attest key
  identifier, used to count requests against a daily and monthly allowance and to
  confirm that requests come from a genuine copy of the app;
- request counters for that allowance;
- short technical logs (time, route, status, duration, model, and a shortened
  one-way hash of the install identifier). **Logs never contain your messages, the
  coach's replies or your training data.**

**Your choices:** you can withdraw permission, or turn health data off, at any time
in the app under Settings > AI Coach. Without permission nothing is sent to the
coach.

## Exercise videos

Some exercises show a video from YouTube. When you play one, it loads from
YouTube's privacy-enhanced domain (youtube-nocookie.com), and Google receives the
information needed to deliver the video, such as your IP address. See
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## What we do not do

- No advertising, no analytics or advertising SDKs, no tracking.
- No sale or sharing of personal data for marketing.
- No use of health data for advertising or data mining.

## Retention and deletion

- Data on your device is removed when you delete the app.
- Data in iCloud can be deleted in the Settings app under your name > iCloud >
  Manage Storage.
- Coach request counters and technical logs on our server contain no message
  content. To ask for anything linked to your install to be deleted, contact us.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under Türkiye's Personal Data Protection
Law (KVKK) and the EU GDPR, you may have the right to access, correct or delete
your personal data and to object to its processing. Contact us to exercise these
rights.

## Changes

We will update this page when the app's data practices change and revise the
effective date above.
