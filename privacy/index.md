**Effective date:** 16 November 2026

Volura ("the app") is developed by Umut Ulaş Demir, an individual developer in
Türkiye ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app uses, where it goes
and the choices you have. Türkçe sürüm: [Gizlilik Politikası](../tr/privacy/).

## Summary

- There is no account to create. We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you
  across other companies' apps and websites.
- Your workouts, programs, profile and measurements are stored on your device and
  in **your own iCloud account**. We cannot see them.
- Your data leaves your device for our server only when you use the **AI Coach**,
  and only after you allow it. Health data is shared with the coach only if you
  turn that on separately.
- The app sends **anonymous usage statistics and crash reports** so we can find
  problems and improve it. They never contain your messages, names, training,
  nutrition, sleep or health data.

## Data stored on your device and in your iCloud

The app stores your profile and profile photo, workouts and logged sets, schedule,
custom workouts and programs, saved content, body measurements, preferences and
the other records you create in the app. This data is kept on your device and
synchronized through Apple's iCloud (the private CloudKit database and iCloud
key-value storage) so it is available on your other devices. It is stored under
your Apple Account and is not accessible to us. Coach conversations are stored
only on your device.

## Apple Health {#apple-health}

With your permission:

- the iPhone app **reads** body weight, body fat percentage and lean body mass to
  keep your progress up to date;
- the iPhone app **reads** sleep analysis, and heart rate and respiratory rate
  recorded during sleep, to show your sleep;
- the Apple Watch app **reads** heart rate and active energy during a workout and
  **saves** your workouts and active energy to Apple Health.

Health data is used only to provide these features. It is never used for
advertising or sold, it is never included in usage statistics or crash reports,
and it is not sent to our server unless you allow the AI Coach to use health data
(see below). You can change these permissions at any time in the Settings app
under Health. Erasing your Volura data does not delete anything from Apple Health.

## AI Coach

The AI Coach answers questions and can adjust your plan. Before your first message
leaves the device, the app asks for your permission.

**What is sent, when you use the coach:** your message and the training data the
coach needs to answer, such as workouts, logged sets, your program, goals and
exercise history. **Body measurements, workout heart rate and active calories are
sent only if you separately allow health data**; this is off unless you turn it on.
In the app you also choose which areas Coach may connect (Training, Sleep,
Nutrition and Body); an area you turn off is never sent to the coach.

**Where it goes:** requests pass through our server, which runs on Cloudflare, and
are forwarded to **OpenAI** to generate the reply. OpenAI processes this data under
its API terms and policies; see
[openai.com/policies](https://openai.com/policies).

**What our server keeps:**

- a random install identifier and, on supported devices, an Apple App Attest key
  identifier, used to count requests against a daily and monthly allowance and to
  confirm that requests come from a genuine copy of the app;
- request counters for that allowance;
- short technical logs (time, route, status, duration, model, and a shortened
  one-way hash of the install identifier). **Logs never contain your messages, the
  coach's replies or your training data.**

**Your choices:** you can withdraw permission, turn health data off or turn off an
area at any time in the app under Settings > Privacy & Intelligence. Without
permission nothing is sent to the coach.

## Usage statistics and crash reports

To find problems and understand which parts of the app work well, the app sends
two kinds of technical information. Neither is used for advertising, sold or
combined with data from other companies.

**Usage statistics (PostHog, servers in the United States):** anonymous events
about how the app is used, such as which screen was opened, which control was
used, how long a screen or action took, whether an action such as finishing a
workout completed, a brief freeze of the app, and whether a coach request
succeeded. Each event carries the app version, iOS version, device model, app
language, whether an Apple Watch is paired, the week the app was installed and a
random identifier created by the analytics library on your device. Events
**never contain** your name, messages, free text you type, or the values of your
training, nutrition, sleep, body or health data, and no personal profile is
created.

**Crash reports (Sentry, servers in the European Union):** when the app crashes or
hits a serious error, a technical report is sent with the code location, app
version, iOS version and device model. Reports contain no personal data, no
screenshots and no error message text.

These reports are not sent from test or development builds. To ask for reports
linked to your install to be deleted, contact us.

## Exercise videos

Some exercises show a video from YouTube. When you play one, it loads from
YouTube's privacy-enhanced domain (youtube-nocookie.com), and Google receives the
information needed to deliver the video, such as your IP address. See
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## What we do not do

- No advertising and no advertising SDKs.
- No tracking across other companies' apps and websites.
- No sale or sharing of personal data for marketing.
- No use of health data for advertising or data mining.

## Export, retention and deletion

- **Export:** in the app under Settings > Data & Privacy > Export your data, you
  can save a copy of the Volura data on this iPhone as a CSV or JSON file.
- **Erase:** Settings > Data & Privacy > Erase all Volura data removes your Volura
  data from this iPhone and from your iCloud account. It does not delete data in
  Apple Health, copies you have exported or shared elsewhere, copies stored
  locally on your other devices, or the usage statistics and crash reports
  described above.
- Data on your device is also removed when you delete the app. Data in iCloud can
  be deleted in the Settings app under your name > iCloud > Manage Storage.
- Coach request counters and technical logs on our server contain no message
  content. To ask for anything linked to your install to be deleted, contact us.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under Türkiye's Personal Data Protection
Law (KVKK) and the EU GDPR, you may have the right to access, correct or delete
your personal data and to object to its processing. You can export and erase your
Volura data in the app; contact us to exercise any other right.
